Musafir (مسافر)

Privacy Policy & Permission Disclosures
Effective Date: November 3, 2026 • Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Musafir ("we", "our", or "the app") was developed by Mohammed Raees Mackdoom as an all-in-one Muslim daily companion and utility application. The core features of Musafir include automatic smartphone silencing upon entering masjids during congregational prayer times, full Quran reader (16-Line and 12-Line Mushaf), Salaah streak & Qadha tracking, live masjid prayer times, Qibla compass finder, digital Tasbeeh counter, and verified halaal restaurant discovery.

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we access, why we require specific Android permissions, and how your data is handled.

🔒 PRIVACY GUARANTEE

Musafir does NOT collect, sell, lease, or transmit your personal information, precise coordinates, or prayer logs to any remote server or third-party marketing agency. All core geofencing, audio adjustments, and prayer counters are computed 100% locally on your device.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Purpose

To provide automatic silencing during prayer times without requiring manual user intervention, Android requires specific permissions. Below is a complete disclosure of each permission and its precise use:

Android Permission Classification Purpose & User Benefit
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Essential Determines proximity to your chosen masjids to trigger silent mode and calculate Qibla direction to the Ka'bah.
ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Essential for Automation Enables geofences to detect when you enter or leave a masjid while the phone is locked in your pocket. Only evaluated during active prayer windows.
ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Do Not Disturb Allows the app to toggle your device between Silent/Vibrate upon entering a masjid and restore Normal ringer volume upon exit.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Displays active silencing status, Friday Al-Kahf reminders, and prayer check notifications (Yes/No response for Salaah streak and Qadha tracking).
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Battery Saver Schedules exact prayer monitoring windows so location services remain dormant outside prayer hours to maximize your battery life.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Persistence Re-registers your active geofences and alarms after your phone restarts so auto-silence continues working without reopening the app.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION		 Reliability Runs high-accuracy location verification for 1 minute when entering a masjid boundary to prevent false triggers while driving past.
📍 DETAILED BACKGROUND LOCATION DISCLOSURE (Google Play Compliance)

Musafir uses Background Location (android.permission.ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) solely to monitor user-selected masjid geofences.

  • No Location Logging: We do not create, store, or transmit a timeline of your movements.
  • Battery-Optimized Windows: Location checks are only activated during configured prayer time windows (e.g. 15 minutes before Jamaat until 30 minutes after). Outside these windows, location monitoring is entirely dormant.
  • On-Device Only: Coordinates are compared against your locally saved masjids inside Android's internal Geofencing API.

3. Third-Party Network Services

Musafir connects to external networks solely to provide public religious data requested by the user:

  • MasjidBoardLive (masjidboardlive.com): Used to fetch public, real-time Jamaat and prayer timetables for supported masjids. No personal user credentials or identifiers are shared.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API: Used when searching for public masjids or halaal restaurants near a queried location.

4. Data Storage & Security

All user data—including custom masjid geofences, Qadha prayer counts, Salaah streaks, Tasbeeh tallies, and Quran bookmarks—is stored locally in private application sandbox storage (Android SharedPreferences and local databases).

This data is sandboxed by the Android Operating System and is inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.

5. Data Deletion & User Control

You maintain complete control over your data:

  • Reset or Edit: You can edit or delete any monitored masjid, clear your Salaah streak, or reset your Qadha count directly from the app interface at any time.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke location or notification permissions anytime via Android Settings > Apps > Musafir > Permissions.
  • Complete Deletion: Uninstalling the application or selecting Clear Data / Clear Storage in Android settings permanently deletes all stored configurations and counters from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Musafir does not address or target anyone under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children or adults.
